About

Quantum information has become a common language across quantum physics. This workshop brings together experts from different fields to identify concrete points of contact between information-theoretic concepts and the dynamics, characterization, and applications of complex quantum systems. The central goal is to bridge the gap between theory and experiment by establishing clear connections between quantum information measures and experimental observables.

The program is intentionally compact and discussion-oriented. We adapt the qmeets format: each topic is jointly presented by a duo of scientists (30+30 min), followed by a moderated plenary discussion (30 min). Alongside invited talks and contributed posters, the programme leaves ample room for questions, informal exchanges, and the development of new collaborations across disciplinary boundaries.

Covered topics

  • Quantum and classical information theory
  • Quantum many-body systems & quantum fields
  • Quantum correlations & entanglement
  • Quantum simulation & classical competitors
  • Quantum metrology & sensing
  • Quantum communication & cryptography
  • Ultracold atoms
  • Quantum optics
  • Rydberg systems
