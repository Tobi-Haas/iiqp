About

Quantum information has become a common language across quantum physics. This workshop brings together a small group of invited speakers to identify concrete points of contact between information-theoretic concepts and the dynamics, characterization, and applications of complex quantum systems, thereby bridging the gaps between theoretical ideas and recent experimental advances.

We adapt the qmeets format: each topic is jointly presented by a duo of scientists. The program is intentionally compact and discussion-oriented. Alongside invited talks, the programme leaves ample room for questions, informal exchanges, and the development of new collaborations across disciplinary boundaries.

Covered topics

  • Quantum correlations & entanglement
  • Quantum many-body systems & quantum fields
  • Quantum information processing technologies
  • Continuous-variable quantum information
  • Open quantum systems
  • Tensor networks
  • Quantum metrology & sensing
  • Quantum communication & cryptography
  • Quantum simulation & computation
  • Quantum foundations
  • Machine learning in quantum physics
