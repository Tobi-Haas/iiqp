About the workshop

Quantum information has become a common language across quantum physics. This workshop brings together a small group of invited speakers to identify concrete points of contact between information-theoretic concepts and the dynamics, structure, and experimental characterization of quantum systems.

The format is intentionally compact and discussion-oriented. Alongside invited talks, the programme leaves ample room for questions, informal exchanges, and the development of new collaborations across disciplinary boundaries.
